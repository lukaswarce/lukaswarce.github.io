Tech Founder · AI Builder · Software Architect

Christian Spana

I’m Christian Spana, known online as lukaswarce.

I build technology products and startups at the intersection of artificial intelligence, software and business.

My background combines software engineering, product development, entrepreneurship and applied research.

Right now I’m building Chaucheros and experimenting with how AI agents and people can work together to execute tasks and goals.

Here I document what I build, what works, what fails and what I learn along the way.

The journey so far

More than 15 years across research, teaching, freelance work and product teams. From computer vision for agriculture to AI for healthcare, B2B fintech and now AI agents.

  1. Jun 2025 – Present

    Lead Architect · Kardot

    • Engineered the platform’s AI core to support 10,000 concurrent transactions with 99.99% uptime.
    • Pioneered multi-agent systems using the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to connect secure B2B tools.
    • Directed a cross-functional team of 5, accelerating the MVP-to-market lifecycle by 40%.
    • Formulated B2B frameworks securing 10+ key partnerships and reducing development costs by 30%.
  2. Jan 2025 – Jun 2025

    Lead Full Stack Developer & AI Architect · Estrategia.IA

    • Orchestrated an AI-driven KPI tracking engine, reducing client planning cycles by 60%.
    • Conceptualized the product roadmap, driving 25% month-over-month growth in B2B engagement.
  3. Jan 2024 – Jan 2025

    Senior Software Engineer · GoPlay!

    • Architected a marketplace using AWS and Node.js, scaling to 1,000 active players.
    • Spearheaded iOS/Android apps processing $10k+ monthly booking volume.
  4. Aug 2022 – Dec 2023

    Software Development Leader · University of Saskatchewan

    • Developed AI models for medical resource allocation, improving appointment accuracy by 25%.
    • Built a cross-platform ultrasound app supporting 500+ annual exams in remote regions.
  5. Feb 2021 – Aug 2022

    Faculty – AI & Cloud Computing · Saskatchewan Polytechnic

    • Taught Cloud Computing and AI with an 80% project success rate.
    • Guided 25 students to professional AWS certifications.
  6. Dec 2019 – Jan 2021

    AI Solutions Developer · Freelance

    • Delivered full-stack solutions for SMEs, increasing restaurant revenue by 30%.
    • Translated business goals into custom AI automation for 5 companies.
  7. Sep 2017 – Sep 2019

    Research Associate & Lead · University of Saskatchewan

    • Improved drone-image processing efficiency by 40% with a hybrid Python/Go platform.
    • Produced a plant phenotyping platform processing 50TB+ of visual data.
  8. Jan 2017 – Dec 2017

    Software Developer & Data Scientist · Ag Exchange Group

    • Deployed grain classification AI models, raising insurance quality accuracy by 20%.
